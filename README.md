          <strong>"Picking the Right Winners? Optimal Policy Learning and Subsidies"</strong> with Armando Rungi. Draft in preparation.
          <p><strong>Abstract.</strong> We study the firm-level impact of German subsidies aimed at energy efficiency and evaluate whether alternative targeting rules could improve policy outcomes. We combine double machine learning with an optimal policy learning framework to estimate ex post heterogeneous treatment effects and derive ex ante optimal allocation rules. Applying a doubly robust learner, we find that subsidies increase capital stock and employment, on average, while documenting substantial heterogeneity across firms, indicating scope for improving targeting. Therefore, we assess two policies, each of which maximizes either employment or value added. We show how the employment-maximizing policy would disproportionately target smaller firms and yield larger job gains, while the value-added-maximizing policy would favor larger firms, leading to greater output and labor productivity..</p>
